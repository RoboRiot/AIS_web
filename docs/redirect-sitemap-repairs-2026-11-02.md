## Evidence and changes

- The public product sitemap returned HTTP 200 but ended mid-URL after 95,824
  bytes, without a closing urlset element. curl reported error 56 (abrupt TLS
  closure). The previous local build contained the complete 2,123,159-byte XML.
  This confirms truncation, not its underlying proxy/server cause. No server
  diagnostics were performed.
- Replace the monolithic response with 100-product sitemap files generated by
  Next.js generateSitemaps. The root index lists each leaf directly, alongside
  the three existing core/service/trailer maps. The former product sitemap URL
  permanently redirects to the root index.
- Cache compact product URLs and modification dates for generation. Catalog
  loading failures and empty catalogs fail rather than returning an empty 200.
- A build gate validates complete XML, canonical hosts, unique URLs, every
  indexed file, product IDs and a 48-KiB response ceiling before promoting the
  local release pointer. Audit helpers now read all indexed leaves serially and
  reject incomplete XML instead of silently accepting partial coverage.
- `/Parts` redirects case-sensitively to `/parts`, retaining query parameters.
  This is middleware-based because Next config redirects are case-insensitive
  and would also match the lowercase destination.
- `/product-category/ct-scanner` and its positive-numbered archive pages go to
  `/parts?modality=CT`. Historical ordering/page positions are not reproduced.
- `/products/10gig-sfp-receiver-and-antenna-assembly-5311645-2` resolves only to
  the verified GE record b73f9b979f2d, part 5311645-2. ID, exact revision and OEM
  guards remain enforced. The product-resolution cache key was advanced.
- The retired web-design article has no equivalent business content and remains
  404. Invented products, the unresolved power-brush part and malformed archive
  pages also remain 404; no blanket homepage redirects were added.
